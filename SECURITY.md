# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in any All Tuner Labs project, please report it responsibly by emailing **security@alltuner.com**.

Please include:

- A description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce (if applicable)
- The affected project and version

## What to expect

- We will acknowledge your report within 7 days
- We will work with you to understand and resolve the issue
- We will credit you in the fix (unless you prefer to remain anonymous)

## Scope

This policy applies to all public repositories under the [alltuner](https://github.com/alltuner) GitHub organization.

## Out of scope

- Vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies (please report those upstream)
- Social engineering attacks
- Denial of service attacks

As a small independent studio, we can't offer a bug bounty program, but we deeply appreciate responsible disclosure and will always credit your contribution.
